Our craft

Chocolate, made slowly.

Seismic began with a simple belief: chocolate tastes better when it is made with patience. We work in small batches, taste obsessively, and let the cocoa lead.

  • Cocoa chosen for character, not just cocoa percentage.
  • Tempered by hand for a clean snap and a glossy finish.
  • Finished in Kathmandu, in batches small enough to fuss over.

The Seismic kitchen, Kathmandu

The Seismic difference

A little more care, in every bite.

No shortcuts, no mystery ingredients, no enormous machines. Just good cocoa, careful hands, and the belief that a small pleasure should still feel extraordinary.

01

Sourced with intention

We look for cocoa with depth, aroma and a story. Every ingredient has to earn its place in the batch.

02

Tempered by hand

Patience is an ingredient. We temper slowly for a beautiful shine, a clean snap and a melt that lingers.

03

Wrapped for the moment

Every bar and box is finished by hand, ready to be given, shared, or kept quietly for yourself. We won't judge.
