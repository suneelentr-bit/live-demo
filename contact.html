    Before you write

    A few helpful answers.

    Can I order chocolate through this website?

    Not in this demo. Seismic Chocolates is a fictional brand and the site is a static front-end prototype. It does not include a cart, online ordering, payment processing or backend. A live site would need a separate ordering and fulfilment setup.

    Do you make custom gift boxes?

    In the world of this brand, yes — gift boxes can be curated around a budget, a favourite flavour or an occasion. In this concept site, the details are illustrative; use the form to imagine the conversation.

    What about allergens and dietary needs?

    Our imaginary chocolates are made in a kitchen that handles milk, nuts, wheat and soy. A real brand would list full ingredients and allergen information for every product, and would confirm suitability before any order.

    Do you work with stockists and wedding planners?

    Yes, in this invented version of the business. Independent stockists, hotels, wedding planners and thoughtful corporate gifting are all welcome to start a conversation. Replace the placeholder email with your real business address when adapting the site.
