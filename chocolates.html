A small ritual

How to enjoy slowly.

01

Keep it cool

Store your chocolate somewhere dry and below 20°C. The fridge is best avoided, unless you like a cloudy bloom.

02

Let it soften

Take a square out a few minutes before eating. A slightly warmer chocolate releases more aroma.

03

Share the good bit

Chocolate tastes better when it is passed around. We recommend a box slightly larger than you need.
